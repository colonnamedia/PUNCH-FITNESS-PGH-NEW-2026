Good To Know

Nutrition Coaching Questions.

Is this a prescribed diet or medical nutrition program?

No. PUNCH provides general wellness education, practical suggestions, habit coaching, and accountability. We do not diagnose or treat medical conditions, prescribe therapeutic diets, or replace care from a physician or registered dietitian.

Do I have to be a PUNCH member?

No. Member and non-member coaching options will be available, with separate pricing when enrollment opens.

What should I track?

Track what you normally eat and drink, along with energy, training or movement, sleep, and notes. The goal is an honest baseline—not a perfect-looking week.

Will you tell me exactly what to eat?

We focus on education, suggestions, routines, and accountability. Your PUNCH Playbook will identify practical habits to work on. For medical nutrition therapy or an individualized therapeutic diet, we will encourage you to work with an appropriately licensed healthcare professional.

Important: PUNCH nutrition coaching is for general education and wellness support only. It is not medical advice, medical nutrition therapy, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a physician or registered dietitian for medical conditions, allergies, eating disorders, pregnancy, medications, or individualized therapeutic nutrition needs.
