Your plan should change as you do. We begin with the full picture, coach the work, and keep adjusting based on what your body and results tell us.

Good To Know

Personal Training Questions.

Do I need to be fit before starting?

No. Your training starts at your current fitness level and builds from there. That is the entire reason to make the plan personal.

Can I train in the gym or at another location?

Both options are available. Train at the PUNCH studio on Greentree Road or ask whether mobile sessions are available for your location and schedule.

How long is a session?

Sessions typically run 45–60 minutes depending on your plan, goals, and coaching format.

Can personal training include boxing?

Yes. Boxing can be used for conditioning, coordination, confidence, and skill development. Your program can also emphasize strength, functional movement, or sport performance.

What happens after I submit the form?

A PUNCH coach will contact you to discuss your goals, training history, schedule, and the best next step. There is no pressure and no commitment from submitting the inquiry.
