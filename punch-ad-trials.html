Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know.

Do I need boxing experience?
Absolutely not. All class formats are built to welcome complete beginners. Coaches walk you through technique from minute one.
What do I need to bring?
Just yourself and athletic wear. For the free workout, boxing gloves are provided. For One Week Unlimited, boxing gloves are available to use during class.
Does One Week Unlimited auto-renew?
No. It is a one-time purchase with no auto-renew. When your 7 days are up, you decide what is next.
How do I book classes during my trial?
Book through the schedule page or the PushPress member portal. Reserve ahead — evening slots fill up fast.
What happens after my trial ends?
Nothing automatically. You will have options to continue with a monthly membership or class pack.
