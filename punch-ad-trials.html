How PUNCH Uses Boxing

A Better Way to
Get Your Cardio In.

Boxing gives your cardio workout a purpose beyond watching the clock. Your coach changes the pace, moving from comfortable combinations to stronger full-body bursts. You choose the effort that works for you.

Walk

Learn the punches, find your rhythm, and begin moving your body at a comfortable pace.

Jog

Keep the combinations flowing and add more movement to build a steady cardio effort.

Run

Move your feet, use your legs and core, and challenge your stamina with a quicker pace.

Sprint

Give a short, powerful full-body effort, then recover before the next round.

Your coach changes the pace. You control your effort.

No. You can start with zero experience. Your coach introduces the basics and helps you work at your fitness level.
What do I need to bring?
Wear comfortable workout clothes and bring water. Loaner gloves are available for your first visit.
Does One Week Unlimited auto-renew?
No. The seven-day trial is a one-time $19.99 purchase and does not auto-renew.
How early should I arrive?
Arrive about 10 minutes early so your coach can meet you and introduce the boxing basics before class.
