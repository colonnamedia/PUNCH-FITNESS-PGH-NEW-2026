Owner · Trainer · Boxing Coach

Anthony Colonna.

My journey started 30 years ago in mixed martial arts — the mats, the ring, and the grind that comes with the fight world. For the last 20 of those years I've been coaching, and somewhere along the way I fell for something bigger than competition: sharing what boxing and fight training can do for everyday people. It started with one client learning to throw a punch. It grew into a gym full of people who found something they actually look forward to — a community where the first-timer and the veteran train in the same room, and everyone leaves better than they walked in.

  • 30+ years in mixed martial arts
  • 20+ years coaching fighters and everyday people
  • Built Punch into a 200+ member community
  • Coaches adults, youth, and senior programs
  • Believes every level belongs in the same room
