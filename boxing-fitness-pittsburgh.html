First-Class Questions

Start With Zero Experience.

Do I need boxing experience?

No. Most members start with none. Arrive about 10 minutes early and a coach will introduce your stance, basic punches, and the heavy bag.

Is this contact boxing?

No. This is boxing for fitness. You punch heavy bags and focus mitts—not other people.

Is boxing really a cardio workout?

Yes. Repeated heavy-bag rounds combine upper-body work, footwork, rotation, and intervals to challenge your cardiovascular conditioning from head to toe.

What do I need for my first class?

Wear comfortable workout clothes, bring water, and arrive early. We have loaner gloves available for your first visit.
