Punch Fitness Self-Guided Workout Policy, Disclaimer & Waiver

Self-Guided Workout Access Punch Fitness may offer scheduled self-guided workout times between 6:00 AM and 9:30 PM. These workouts are not coached classes. A staff member may or may not be present during all self-guided workout times. Members must schedule their self-guided workout time in advance. Access is only permitted during the scheduled time block. Required Intro Before Access Before using self-guided workouts, every participant must complete either:

  1. A Punch Fitness intro session, or
  2. One approved Punch Fitness class.

This is required so participants understand the facility, equipment, basic expectations, and safety rules. No Coaching or Movement Instruction Self-guided workouts are completed independently. Punch Fitness provides the workout, timer, and general workout format through the app or QR code system. By participating, the member understands that:

  • No coach may be present to demonstrate movements.
  • No coach may be present to correct form.
  • The member is responsible for choosing appropriate weights, movements, intensity, and modifications.
  • The member should stop immediately if they feel pain, dizziness, shortness of breath, or unsafe discomfort.

Members may not share door codes, allow unauthorized people inside, or bring guests without approval from Punch Fitness. Violation of this policy may result in loss of self-guided access or membership termination. Equipment Use & Cleanup Policy Members are responsible for getting out their own equipment and putting all equipment away after use. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Dumbbells
  • Kettlebells
  • Barbells

  • Bikes
  • Mats
  • Boxing equipment
  • Any other equipment used during the workout

Repeated violations may result in loss of self-guided workout access.
